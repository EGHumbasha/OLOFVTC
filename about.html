Who We Are

Empowering Skills, Building Futures

Our Lady of Fatima Vocational Training Centre is dedicated to equipping students with practical skills that empower them to build sustainable livelihoods and contribute meaningfully to society. We offer a diverse range of hands-on training programs designed to prepare learners for employment, entrepreneurship, and community development.

Established with a vision of transforming lives through education, our institution promotes excellence, discipline, and creativity — guided by Christian values and a strong commitment to service.

Our Mission

To provide quality vocational and technical training that empowers students with life-changing skills, enabling them to become self-reliant, responsible citizens who contribute positively to their communities.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of excellence in vocational education and training, nurturing creativity, innovation, and entrepreneurship for a better and more productive society.

Our Core Values

Faith, Integrity, Excellence, Service, Discipline, and Compassion — these values guide our teaching, learning, and community engagement.

Our History

A short history of Our Lady of Fatima Vocational Training Centre and its journey toward community impact and skills development.

1998 — Foundation

Our Lady of Fatima Vocational Training Centre was established to offer practical skills training to youth and women in the local community.

2005 — Program Expansion

The centre expanded its programs to include catering, bakery studies, and cutting & designing in response to community demand.

2015 — Facilities Upgrade

Major investment in modern workshops, computer labs, and a resource centre improved the quality of hands-on instruction.

2023 — Community Partnerships

Partnered with local businesses and NGOs to support internships, placements, and community outreach projects.

Accreditation & Partnerships

Our Lady of Fatima Vocational Training Centre is committed to high standards of teaching and learning. We are registered with relevant national training authorities and maintain working relationships with local businesses, churches, and NGOs to ensure our curriculum remains relevant and provides real pathways to employment.

Key Partners: Local industry partners, community churches, microfinance institutions, and development NGOs.

Achievements & Impact

Over the years, we have:

  • Trained hundreds of graduates now running small enterprises.
  • Supported job placements and internships with local employers.
  • Hosted community outreach and skills days benefiting neighboring areas.

We continuously document graduate success stories and track outcomes to improve our programs.
