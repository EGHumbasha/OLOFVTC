About Us

Welcome to Our Lady of Fatima Vocational Training Centre

Our mission is to empower youth and adults with practical skills for employability and entrepreneurship. We provide a range of vocational courses designed to equip students with real-world experience.

Our programs include bakery, cutting and design, home decor, events decor, hairdressing, and carpentry. Students learn from experienced instructors and gain hands-on training.
